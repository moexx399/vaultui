## v0.1.0 - 2026-10-28
### Added
* Initial release: a Bubble Tea TUI that finds a user's Vault identity entities by email or username and browses the paths their group policies declare, with a fuzzy-filtered path list, per-path rule previews with source policies and groups, and a full report that merges identical permission bodies and flags unreadable or unconsolidated policies. Contexts and logins come from vaultctx.
