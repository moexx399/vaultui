## v0.3.0 - 2026-10-28
### Added
* replication-status follows replication: every DR and performance peer a reachable cluster reports is checked too (health, replication state, WAL lag and TLS certificate), round by round, using the TLS settings of the context it was found through, and shown as discovered via that context.
### Fixed
* replication-status no longer warns about DR secondary clusters: the 472 that the active node of a DR secondary returns from sys/health is healthy. Standby and performance standby nodes still warn.

## v0.2.1 - 2026-10-28
### Dependencies
* Bump the go_modules group across 1 directory with 2 updates
* Bump golang.org/x/crypto from 0.45.0 to 0.52.0

## v0.2.0 - 2026-10-28
### Added
* Initial release: vaultui, a Bubble Tea TUI for Vault built on vaultctx contexts, with a menu of functions that can also be run directly as `vaultui FUNCTION`. user-policies finds a user's identity entities by email or username and browses the paths their group policies declare, with a fuzzy-filtered path list, per-path rule previews with source policies and groups, and a full report that merges identical permission bodies and flags unreadable or unconsolidated policies. replication-status checks every vaultctx context without logging in and shows each replication topology as a tab: node health, DR and performance primary/secondary trees with WAL lag and link heartbeats, unreachable contexts, and TLS certificate expiry and consistency.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-10-28
### Added
* Initial release: a Bubble Tea TUI that finds a user's Vault identity entities by email or username and browses the paths their group policies declare, with a fuzzy-filtered path list, per-path rule previews with source policies and groups, and a full report that merges identical permission bodies and flags unreadable or unconsolidated policies. Contexts and logins come from vaultctx.
