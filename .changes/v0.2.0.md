## v0.2.0 - 2026-10-28
### Added
* Initial release: vaultui, a Bubble Tea TUI for Vault built on vaultctx contexts, with a menu of functions that can also be run directly as `vaultui FUNCTION`. user-policies finds a user's identity entities by email or username and browses the paths their group policies declare, with a fuzzy-filtered path list, per-path rule previews with source policies and groups, and a full report that merges identical permission bodies and flags unreadable or unconsolidated policies. replication-status checks every vaultctx context without logging in and shows each replication topology as a tab: node health, DR and performance primary/secondary trees with WAL lag and link heartbeats, unreachable contexts, and TLS certificate expiry and consistency.
